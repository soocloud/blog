## Intro
Overcoming a challenge feels great, but spending time with family feels even more special.

Today, I participated in the Spartan Race held at German Village in Chiba. I joined the "Super" which is a 10 km course with 25 obstacles.

Although the actual race started at 9:30 a.m., our journey began much ealier, at 4:30 a.m..Since we do not have a car, it took us about 2 hours to get to the venue.

My wife did not participate in the race. Running has never really been her thing, and more importantly, she was busy taking care of our lovely eight-month-old daughter. Still, she was just as busy as I was that morning, preparing our daughter's meals, clothes, and other essentials. Meanwhile, I cuddled my baby while checking my gear and plans(?) for what would become the toughest race of my life so far.

At 6:00 a.m., we left home. It was already getting hot, but all of us were excited. For me, it was the race. For my wife, and hopefully for my daughter too, it was a little family adventure to a small seaside town in Chiba overlooking the Pacific Ocean.
